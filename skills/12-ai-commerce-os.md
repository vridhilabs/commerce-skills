# Skill: AI Commerce OS (Phase 12)

## Overview
Phase 12 deploys autonomous AI Role Managers that continuously monitor metrics, execute operational tasks, and optimize decisions.

---

## 8 Autonomous AI Role Managers
1. **AI Procurement Manager**: Automated purchase orders based on predictive safety stock and vendor lead times.
2. **AI Warehouse Manager**: Dynamic bin allocation optimization based on pick frequency (fast-movers at front).
3. **AI Dispatcher**: Real-time rider dispatch solver adjusting for weather, traffic, and order weight constraints.
4. **AI Pricing Engine**: Automated margin-preserving dynamic pricing and FEFO expiry markdown curves.
5. **AI Marketing Manager**: Generates hyper-personalized ad copy and automatically pauses underperforming campaign channels.
6. **AI Finance Assistant**: Bank payout reconciliation, GST anomaly detection, and commission split auditing.
7. **AI Customer Support**: 24/7 autonomous issue resolution for order tracking, cancellations, and instant refunds.
8. **AI Business Advisor**: Natural language executive command center providing instant strategic insights.
