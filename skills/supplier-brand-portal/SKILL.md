# Skill: Supplier & Brand Platform (Phase 3)

## Overview
Phase 3 opens the platform up to third-party suppliers, D2C brands, and manufacturers, establishing a multi-vendor marketplace infrastructure.

---

## 1. Vendor Portal
- **Vendor Onboarding**: Automated KYB (Know Your Business), tax GSTIN verification, bank account validation, and contract signing.
- **Product Catalog Management**: CSV/XLSX bulk product uploader, variant setup, image asset manager, and price list approvals.
- **Inventory Sync**: API and SFTP inventory feed sync with vendor ERP systems (SAP, Tally, Zoho).
- **Settlement & Performance**: Automated weekly payout statements, commission deduction summary, SLA fulfillment scoring.

## 2. Brand Portal
- **Brand Analytics Dashboard**: Real-time sales telemetry, SKU market share, geographic heatmaps, sell-through rates.
- **Promotional Campaigns**: Sponsored product placements, banner bidding system, flash sale participation requests.
- **Inventory Forecasting**: Predictive demand signals sent to brands to optimize production schedules.
