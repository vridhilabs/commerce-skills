# Skill: API & Ecosystem Integrations (Phase 11)

## Overview
Phase 11 connects Commerce OS to the broader global e-commerce, ERP, accounting, marketing, payment, and logistics ecosystem.

---

## Supported Ecosystem Connectors
- **E-Commerce Platforms**: Shopify, WooCommerce, Medusa.js, Magento.
- **ERPs & Accounting**: ERPNext, Tally Prime, Zoho Books, SAP Business One.
- **Payment Gateways**: Razorpay, PhonePe, Paytm, Stripe, UPI.
- **Logistics & Aggregators**: Shiprocket, Delhivery, Porter, Dunzo.
- **Open Protocols**: ONDC (Open Network for Digital Commerce) buyer/seller node integration.
- **Channels & Ads**: WhatsApp Business API, Meta Ads, Google Shopping Ads, Amazon, Flipkart, Blinkit Seller APIs.

# Skill: AI Commerce OS (Phase 12)

## Overview
Phase 12 introduces autonomous AI Role Managers that act as digital staff operating WMS, OMS, dispatch, pricing, marketing, and finance.

---

## Autonomous AI Role Managers
1. **AI Procurement Manager**: Monitors inventory velocity, places POs with suppliers automatically when stock hits reorder thresholds.
2. **AI Warehouse Manager**: Optimizes bin placement based on picking co-occurrence, assigns wave picking queues to pickers.
3. **AI Dispatcher**: Real-time rider matching, dynamic routing adjustment based on traffic, rider workload balancing.
4. **AI Dynamic Pricing Engine**: Real-time margin protection, competitor price benchmarking, automated near-expiry markdowns.
5. **AI Marketing Manager**: Generates targeted WhatsApp/push copy, runs AB tests, auto-pauses low ROI campaigns.
6. **AI Finance Assistant**: Reconciles payment gateway payouts against bank statements, flags suspicious chargebacks.
7. **AI Customer Support Agent**: Autonomous resolution of missing item refunds, delivery status inquiries, address updates.
8. **AI Business Advisor**: Natural language executive assistant analyzing cross-channel GMV, margin leaks, growth levers.
