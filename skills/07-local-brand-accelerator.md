# Skill: Local Brand Accelerator (Phase 7)

## Overview
Phase 7 accelerates regional D2C brands, local artisans, and new manufacturers with generative studio tools, packaging assistance, and white-label sourcing.

---

## 1. Brand Success Studio
- **AI Product Studio**: Generative AI backgrounds and studio lighting generation for raw product photos.
- **AI Copywriter**: Automated SEO-optimized titles, bullet points, meta descriptions, and ingredient/allergen callouts.
- **Creator & Affiliate Marketplace**: Connect local brands with micro-creators for product sampling and review generation.

## 2. White Label & OEM Sourcing
- **Private Label Launchpad**: Connect brands to verified OEM manufacturers for custom contract manufacturing.
- **Quality Audit Engine**: Standardized quality check scorecards for incoming private label goods.
- **Packaging Customization**: Standardized eco-friendly custom packaging templates and low-MOQ print ordering.

# Skill: B2B Commerce (Phase 8)

## Overview
Phase 8 opens bulk ordering, net-30 credit terms, team accounts, and standing orders for business buyers across 7 key commercial verticals.

---

## Target B2B Customers
1. **Restaurants**: Fresh produce, oils, dairy, sauces, packaging containers.
2. **Hotels**: Linen, toiletries, bulk breakfast supplies, cleaning chemicals.
3. **Cafés**: Specialty coffee beans, syrups, milks, bakery items.
4. **Offices**: Snacks, coffee pods, paper supplies, IT accessories.
5. **Salons**: Professional hair care, towels, sanitizers, beauty consumables.
6. **Hospitals**: Non-pharma medical consumables, gloves, bedsheets, sanitization supplies.
7. **Retail Stores**: Wholesale FMCG replenishment, display units.

## Core Features
- **Bulk Tiered Pricing**: Quantity-based pricing matrix (1-10 units, 11-50 units, 50+ units).
- **Credit Terms (Net 15/30/60)**: Credit check scoring, approval workflow, and automated payment reminders.
- **GST Invoicing**: Multi-GSTIN account management for corporate entities.
- **Approval Workflows**: Procurement manager order drafting with executive sign-off thresholds.
