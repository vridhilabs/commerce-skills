# Skill: Commerce Platform SDK & APIs (Phase 13)

## Overview
Phase 13 transforms the internal Commerce OS into an open developer platform allowing third-party developers, agencies, and enterprises to extend the platform.

---

## Technical Capabilities
- **Public REST & GraphQL APIs**: Fully documented OpenAPI 3.0 / GraphQL schemas for Catalog, Inventory, Orders, Fleet, and Finance.
- **Real-time Webhooks Engine**: Subsecond event notifications (`order.created`, `stock.low`, `shipment.delivered`, `payout.processed`).
- **SDK Suite**: Official Client SDKs in TypeScript, Python, Go, Swift, and Kotlin.
- **Plugin Marketplace**: Developer Portal allowing third-party apps to monetize plugins (custom themes, specialized ERP connectors, custom carrier drivers).
- **Multi-Tenant Architecture**: Complete tenant isolation, custom domain mapping, dedicated database schemas, and white-label branding controls.

# Skill: Vertical Marketplaces (Niche Specs)

## Overview
18 turnkey niche vertical marketplace templates pre-configured with custom tax rules, delivery SLA targets, storage requirements, and product schemas.

---

## 18 Launchable Verticals
1. 🥗 **Healthy Foods Club**: Cold-chain compliance, organic certification badges, macro-nutrient filters.
2. 🌾 **Farmer Marketplace**: Direct farm-to-door aggregation, harvest date tracking, crop grade pricing.
3. 🏠 **Home Essentials Club**: Bulk cleaning/paper, subscription auto-refill, household size calculators.
4. 🍼 **Baby Club**: Expiry-locked formula tracking, safety certification tags, milestone-based subscription boxes.
5. 🐶 **Pet Club**: Pet breed/age profile customizer, heavy bag delivery routing, vet prescription upload.
6. 💄 **Beauty Club**: Shade finders, ingredient allergen filters, high-value theft security pick workflows.
7. ☕ **Coffee & Tea Club**: Roast date freshness guarantee, grind size selection, bean origin story cards.
8. 🧘 **Wellness Club**: Batch purity COA lab reports, supplement stack builders, recurring wellness plans.
9. 🛠 **Hardware Club**: Technical specs filter, contractor bulk pricing, job site delivery pin dropping.
10. 🚗 **Auto Parts Club**: Vehicle VIN number search lookup, OEM part compatibility check, fast garage dispatch.
11. ⚡ **EV Parts Marketplace**: Battery health grade certificates, charger compatibility, technician booking.
12. 🏢 **Office Supplies**: Corporate GST billing, department spend caps, recurring pantry replenishment.
13. 🍽 **Restaurant Supply**: Nightly produce ordering (11 PM cutoff for 5 AM delivery), temperature-logged trucks.
14. 🏥 **Medical Consumables**: Batch sterilisation records, FDA/CDSCO license validation, emergency express delivery.
15. 🧹 **Cleaning Supplies**: Industrial chemical safety data sheets (MSDS), bulk drum vs spray bottle packaging.
16. 🏗 **Construction Materials**: Truck payload weight calculations, crane delivery scheduling, site manager OTP handoff.
17. 🎨 **Local Artisan Marketplace**: Single-item inventory locks, artisan story videos, custom gift packaging.
18. 🛍 **D2C Brand Marketplace**: Multi-brand shared dark store fulfillment, cross-brand bundling rewards.
