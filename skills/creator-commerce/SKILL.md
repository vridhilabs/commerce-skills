# Skill: Creator Commerce (Phase 9)

## Overview
Phase 9 enables influencers, content creators, and curators to launch custom storefronts, run live shopping events, and earn commissions.

---

## Key Features
- **Creator Storefronts**: Custom branded sub-domains or in-app micro-shops curated by creators.
- **Affiliate & Commission Engine**: Unique tracking links, multi-tier attribution, real-time commission ledger.
- **Live Shopping**: Interactive live stream video player with pinned product cards and 1-click cart addition.
- **Product Tagging**: Tag catalog items in video reels and stories with direct quick-commerce checkout.
