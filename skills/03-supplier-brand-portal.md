# Skill: Supplier & Brand Platform (Phase 3)

## Overview
Phase 3 opens the platform up to third-party suppliers, D2C brands, and manufacturers, establishing a multi-vendor marketplace infrastructure.

---

## 1. Vendor Portal
- **Vendor Onboarding**: Automated KYB (Know Your Business), tax GSTIN verification, bank account validation, and contract signing.
- **Product Catalog Management**: CSV/XLSX bulk product uploader, variant setup, image asset manager, and price list approvals.
- **Inventory Sync**: API and SFTP inventory feed sync with vendor ERP systems (SAP, Tally, Zoho).
- **Settlement & Performance**: Automated weekly payout statements, commission deduction summary, SLA fulfillment scoring.

## 2. Brand Portal
- **Brand Analytics Dashboard**: Real-time sales telemetry, SKU market share, geographic heatmaps, sell-through rates.
- **Promotional Campaigns**: Sponsored product placements, banner bidding system, flash sale participation requests.
- **Inventory Forecasting**: Predictive demand signals sent to brands to optimize production schedules.

---

# Skill: Customer Growth & CRM (Phase 4)

## Overview
Phase 4 powers customer retention, lifetime value (LTV) maximization, dynamic marketing automation, and recurring subscription revenue engines.

---

## 1. Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
- **Unified Customer Profiles**: 360-degree customer view tracking order history, favorite categories, SLA satisfaction rate, and support tickets.
- **RFM Segmentation**: Recency, Frequency, Monetary value customer clustering for automated targeted campaigns.
- **Wallet & Loyalty**: Integrated digital wallet balance, cashback reward tiers, referral codes, and gamified streak bonuses.

## 2. Marketing Engine
- **Omnichannel Campaigns**: Multi-channel campaign runner across WhatsApp Business API, Push Notifications (FCM), SMS, and Email (SendGrid).
- **Automated Re-engagement**: Abandoned cart triggers (15-min WhatsApp nudge), win-back sequences for dormant buyers.

## 3. Subscription Engine
- **Recurring Orders**: Scheduled deliveries (daily milk, weekly veggies, monthly boxes), flexible skip/pause controls, and recurring billing via auto-debit APIs.
