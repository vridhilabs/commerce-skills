# Skill: Financial OS (Phase 6)

## Overview
Phase 6 builds a bulletproof financial infrastructure handling payments, invoicing, tax compliance, vendor/rider payouts, commission rules, and embedded fintech lending.

---

## 1. Finance Engine
- **Payment Split Gateway**: Split payment transactions at checkout between Marketplace commission, Dark Store operator fee, Fleet driver payout, and Vendor balance.
- **Automated Tax & GST**: HSN/SAC code automated lookup, CGST/SGST/IGST breakdown, GST-compliant e-way bill generation, and credit note issuance.
- **Payout Settlement**: T+1 vendor payout reconciliation, instant daily driver cashout via UPI, and refund ledger auditing.

## 2. Embedded Commerce Lending
- **Inventory Financing**: Capital loans provided to vendors against verified dark store stock levels.
- **Invoice Discounting**: Immediate payout to B2B suppliers for 30-day corporate invoice orders.
- **Fleet Asset Finance**: Micro-financing for riders purchasing EV delivery vehicles.
