# Skill: B2B Commerce (Phase 8)

## Overview
Phase 8 opens bulk ordering, net-30 credit terms, team accounts, and standing orders for business buyers across 7 key commercial verticals.

---

## Target B2B Verticals
1. **Restaurants**: Fresh produce, oils, dairy, sauces, eco-packaging.
2. **Hotels**: Linen, bulk breakfast items, guest amenities, sanitization.
3. **Cafés**: Specialty coffee, syrups, dairy/alt-milks, bakery goods.
4. **Offices**: Pantry snacks, coffee machines, paper products, IT supplies.
5. **Salons**: Professional cosmetics, towels, single-use aprons, sanitizers.
6. **Hospitals**: Gloves, bandages, non-pharma consumables, hygiene.
7. **Retail Stores**: Wholesale FMCG replenishment, display units.

## Core Capabilities
- **Bulk Tiered Pricing & Quotes**: Volume discounts, custom quote requests, contract pricing.
- **Credit Terms & Risk Scoring**: Net 15/30/60 approval workflows with credit limit enforcement.
- **Corporate Team Accounts**: Multi-user procurement with spend limits and manager approvals.
- **Standing Orders**: Automated weekly/monthly replenishment schedules.
