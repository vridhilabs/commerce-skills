# Skill: Local Brand Accelerator (Phase 7)

## Overview
Phase 7 accelerates regional D2C brands, local artisans, and new manufacturers with generative studio tools, packaging assistance, and white-label sourcing.

---

## 1. Brand Success Studio
- **AI Product Studio**: Generative AI backgrounds and studio lighting generation for raw product photos.
- **AI Copywriter**: Automated SEO-optimized titles, bullet points, meta descriptions, and ingredient/allergen callouts.
- **Creator & Affiliate Marketplace**: Connect local brands with micro-creators for product sampling and review generation.

## 2. White Label & OEM Sourcing
- **Private Label Launchpad**: Connect brands to verified OEM manufacturers for custom contract manufacturing.
- **Quality Audit Engine**: Standardized quality check scorecards for incoming private label goods.
- **Packaging Customization**: Standardized eco-friendly custom packaging templates and low-MOQ print ordering.
