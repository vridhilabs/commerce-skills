# Skill: Customer Growth & CRM (Phase 4)

## Overview
Phase 4 powers customer retention, lifetime value (LTV) maximization, dynamic marketing automation, and recurring subscription revenue engines.

---

## 1. CRM & Customer Profiles
- **Unified Customer 360**: Real-time aggregated customer timeline: purchases, browsing history, favorite categories, support interactions, and delivery address clusters.
- **Dynamic RFM Segmentation**: Automatic grouping by Recency, Frequency, and Monetary metrics (e.g. VIP, At-Risk, Loyal, New Spenders).
- **Wallet & Rewards Engine**: Digital store wallet, cashback rules, referral reward links, and loyalty point conversion logic.

## 2. Marketing Automation
- **Multi-channel Execution**: Orchestrate campaigns via WhatsApp Business API, Firebase Push Notifications, SMS, and Email.
- **Triggered Workflows**: Abandoned cart recovery (15-min WhatsApp nudge), post-purchase feedback loops, and re-order reminders.

## 3. Subscription Engine
- **Scheduled Deliveries**: Recurring daily/weekly/monthly subscriptions (milk, coffee, fresh produce, pet food).
- **Billing & Handoff**: Auto-debit via UPI Recurring / Credit Card mandates, automated pick-list generation for dark stores every morning at 4:00 AM.
