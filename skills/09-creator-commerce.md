# Skill: Creator Commerce (Phase 9)

## Overview
Phase 9 enables influencers, content creators, and curators to launch custom storefronts, run live shopping events, and earn commissions.

---

## Key Features
- **Creator Storefronts**: Custom branded sub-domains or in-app micro-shops curated by creators.
- **Affiliate & Commission Engine**: Unique tracking links, multi-tier attribution, real-time commission ledger.
- **Live Shopping**: Interactive live stream video player with pinned product cards and 1-click cart addition.
- **Product Tagging**: Tag catalog items in video reels and stories with direct quick-commerce checkout.

---

# Skill: Hyperlocal Network & Franchising (Phase 10)

## Overview
Phase 10 turns physical dark stores into a decentralized franchise network with shared operations and revenue models.

---

## Key Features
- **Partner Warehouse Onboarding**: Self-serve registration for local store owners to convert space into hyper-local dark stores.
- **Capacity & SLA Scoring**: Real-time bin utilization telemetry, picking SLA performance scoring, order throughput metrics.
- **Franchise Dashboard**: Complete operator dashboard for inventory overview, rider queue monitoring, daily revenue share analytics.
